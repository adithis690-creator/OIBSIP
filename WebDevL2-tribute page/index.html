Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

The "Missile Man of India" and the People's 11th President

Timeline of an Inspiring Life

  • 1931 – Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, India. Raised in a modest family, selling newspapers to supplement his family's income.
  • 1954 – Graduated in Physics from Saint Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli.
  • 1960 – Completed aerospace engineering from the Madras Institute of Technology (MIT) and joined DRDO as a scientist.
  • 1969 – Transferred to ISRO, serving as the project director of India's first satellite launch vehicle (SLV-III), which deployed the Rohini satellite in 1980.
  • 1992 - 1999 – Served as the Chief Scientific Adviser to the Prime Minister and Secretary of the DRDO, playing a key role in the Pokhran-II nuclear tests.
  • 1997 – Awarded the **Bharat Ratna**, India's highest civilian honor, for his vast contributions to scientific research and modernization of defense technology.
  • 2002 - 2007 – Served as the **11th President of India**, widely revered as the "People's President."
  • 2015 – Passed away on July 27th while doing what he loved most—delivering a lecture to students at IIM Shillong.
